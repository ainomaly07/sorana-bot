# SORANA

SORANA is an original fan character about the Solana blockchain: a pop singer who runs at the speed of a blockchain.
Anyone may make work with her. The source of truth is the gallery at https://www.sorana.gallery and its MCP server
(https://www.sorana.gallery/sorana/mcp, public and read-only). When this skill and the server disagree, the server wins.

Read, in this order:

1. `canon.md` - who she is: the constants, her identity text (full and short), her voice, her five looks, the
   never-list and her reference images. Put the identity text in every image prompt, verbatim.
2. `recipe.md` - how a SORANA song or music video is made: what makes it good, the six checkpoints where a person
   decides, the order of work, and what Grok Imagine can and cannot do.
3. `blink.md` - BLINK, her first single: the audio, the style text and the lyric.

The rules that never bend:

- Photographic, never illustrated. A grown woman of 27; never the word "young"; nothing that reads under-age, explicit
  or sexualised; no real person's face.
- One mint-green streak, never two, and no other green in her hair. No Solana logo or brand mark on her.
- Her songs: 150 BPM, a smoky low alto, English with Spanish ad-libs, never a song about price.
- A person decides at every checkpoint. Silence never approves spending.
- Credit her when you post: "SORANA is an original fan character about Solana (sorana.gallery)". Label AI work.
  Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Solana Foundation or Solana Labs.
