# SORANA: the canon

SORANA keeps her own time. She counts in beats of 400 milliseconds, charges a fraction of a cent, has gone dark a few times and came back brighter every time. She is fast, warm and a little cocky, and she never sleeps. Her clothes change with every world; her head never does.

## The constants
- Hair: Very long, glossy and straight, violet (#9945FF), with a centre part and soft curtain bangs. It moves when she does.
- Streak: One mint-green streak (#14F195) down the front lock on her left side (the viewer's right in a front view). Never two, and no other green in her hair.
- Liner: A sharp mint-green eyeliner wing on each eye. It is the first thing you see when she blinks.
- Bolt: A small flat mint-green lightning-bolt earring on her left ear. Speed is her whole thing.
- Face: Warm tan skin, hazel-green eyes, full lips, a beauty mark under her left eye. She rests in a half-smile. When she sings, her mouth opens wide and her eyes close.
- Age: A grown woman of 27, with an adult face and high cheekbones. Prompt her age and never the word "young".
- Colour: Violet and mint are hers. Each look brings its own colours; on her head, mint is the one accent.
- Medium: Photographic, never illustrated: film stills with grain and stage light.

## Her identity, short (for video prompts, where the first frame carries her)
a 27-year-old woman with very long straight violet hair, one mint-green streak, mint eyeliner wings and a mint lightning-bolt earring

## Her voice
- Tone: A smoky low alto, close to the mic, with breath in every line.
- Delivery: Half-spoken verses that ride the kick, then sung hooks stacked with whispered doubles.
- Tempo: 150 BPM, always. One beat every 400 ms, the length of a Solana slot.
- Words: English, with Spanish ad-libs: dale, uno, dos, sol.
- Sound: Jersey club kicks and dembow under glassy Y2K trance arps.
- Never: A high squeaky vocal, a slow ballad, or a song about price.
- Style text: Jersey club kicks and dembow percussion under a glassy Y2K trance supersaw arpeggio, 150 BPM, rubbery sub bass, smoky low alto female lead vocal, breathy, confident, close-mic, half-spoken verses, sung hooks with stacked whispered doubles, Spanish ad-libs
- Exclude: male vocals, high squeaky vocals, guitars, slow ballad

## Never
- Two streaks, or any other green in her hair.
- The word "young" in a prompt.
- An illustrated or anime version as her canon.
- The Solana logo, or any brand mark, on her.
- A real person's face.
- Price talk or promises in her lyrics.
